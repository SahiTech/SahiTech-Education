# MDI Domain Models & Academic Engines

## 1. Domain Overview
The Madrasa Digitalization Initiative (MDI) encapsulates distinct pedagogical systems within a unified domain-driven architecture.

---

## 2. Qawmi Academic Engine (Dars-e-Nizami)

### 2.1 Classical Marhala Hierarchy
1. **Atfal / Nurani**: Early childhood literacy, basic Quranic recitation, Masnoon du'as.
2. **Hifzul Quran**: Memorization of the 30 Juz (Paras), Sabak, Sabki, Ammokhta tracking.
3. **Ibtidaiyyah (Primary)**: Basic Arabic, Urdu, Persian, Bengali, Elementary Arithmetic.
4. **Mutawassitah (Middle)**: Nahw (Syntax), Sarf (Morphology), Fiqh basics (Qudoori).
5. **Sanawiyyah Aammah (Secondary)**: Intermediate Arabic literature, Usul al-Fiqh, Balaghah.
6. **Sanawiyyah Khassah (Higher Secondary)**: Advanced Fiqh (Hidayah), Tafseer (Jalalayn), Logic.
7. **Fazilat (Bachelor level)**: Mishkat al-Masabih, comparative Fiqh.
8. **Takmil / Dawra-e-Hadith (Master level equivalent)**: Kutub al-Sittah (Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Nasa'i, Ibn Majah).
9. **Takhassus / Ifta**: Post-graduate specialization in Islamic Jurisprudence, Hadith, or Arabic Literature.

### 2.2 Grading & Evaluation
- **Mumtaz (Distinction)**: 80% - 100%
- **Jayyid Jiddan (Very Good)**: 65% - 79%
- **Jayyid (Good)**: 50% - 64%
- **Maqbool (Pass)**: 33% - 49%
- **Rasib (Fail)**: Below 33%

## 3. Alia Academic Engine (BMEB Framework)

### 3.1 Grade Levels
1. **Ebtedayee**: Grades 1 to 5
2. **Dakhil**: Grades 6 to 10 (Equivalent to SSC)
3. **Alim**: Grades 11 and 12 (Equivalent to HSC)
4. **Fazil**: Undergraduate Degree
5. **Kamil**: Postgraduate Degree

### 3.2 Evaluation & Grading
- Bangladesh Madrasa Education Board (BMEB) GPA System (Grade Point 0 to 5.0).
- Standard grading scale: A+ (5.00), A (4.00), A- (3.50), B (3.00), C (2.00), D (1.00), F (0.00).

---

## 4. Trilingual Field Standardization
All domain entities support localization across three languages:
- **Bangla (`bn`)**: Primary administrative and instructional medium in Bangladesh.
- **English (`en`)**: International communication, technical reporting, and export formats.
- **Arabic (`ar`)**: Classical terminology, Sanad certificates, Islamic jurisprudence terms, and Quranic/Hadith titles.
