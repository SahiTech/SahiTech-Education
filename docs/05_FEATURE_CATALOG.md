# 05. Complete Platform Feature Catalog

**Program**: Madrasa Digitalization Initiative (MDI)  
**Parent Brand**: SahiTech  
**Division**: SahiTech Education  

---

## 1. Feature Breakdown by Domain Context

```
1. Tenancy, Governance & Platform Administration
   ├── Multi-Tenant Provisioning (Automated Tenant & Branch Setup)
   ├── Institutional Profile Management (Contact, Address, Logo, Hierarchy)
   ├── Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) with Granular Matrix
   ├── Custom Domain & Subdomain Routing Engine (Cloudflare Edge Integration)
   └── Tamper-Evident Immutable Audit Log (User Actions & System Events)

2. User & Identity Management
   ├── Supabase Auth Integration (Email/Password, Phone OTP)
   ├── User Profile Management (Trilingual Names, Phone, Avatar)
   ├── Session Security & Device Concurrency Tracking
   └── Privacy-Compliant Guardian-to-Student Mapping

3. Academic Management (Dual-Engine Core)
   ├── Academic Year & Calendar Lifecycle (Hijri + Gregorian with Moon Sighting Offset)
   ├── Qawmi Curriculum Engine (Dars-e-Nizami, 9 Marhalas, Classical Kitabs)
   ├── Alia Curriculum Engine (BMEB Framework, Grades 1 to Kamil, GPA Scale)
   ├── Hybrid Mode Support for Dual-Wing Madrasas
   ├── Department, Class, Section, and Subject Hierarchy
   └── Weekly Class Routine & Teacher Timetable Scheduling

4. Student Information System (SIS)
   ├── Digital Admission Pipeline & Application Review
   ├── Comprehensive Student Biographical & Residential Registry
   ├── Historical Class Enrollments & Multi-Year Academic Progression
   ├── Document Vault (Birth registration, certificates, photos)
   └── Validated Bulk Student Import & Export (Excel/CSV)

5. Faculty & Staff Human Resources
   ├── Faculty & Staff Profiles with Sanad & Board Credentials
   ├── Teacher Subject Allocation & Weekly Lecture Workload Meter
   ├── Staff Attendance & Leave Tracking
   └── Salary Structure & Basic Payroll Preparation

6. Attendance & Biometric Integration
   ├── Daily, Session, and Period-Wise Student Attendance
   ├── Low-Bandwidth Mobile PWA Attendance Marking
   ├── Hardware Webhook Adapter for Biometric Terminals (ZKTeco)
   └── Automated Absentee Alert Notification Dispatch (SMS/Push)

7. Examination, Evaluation & Sanad Engine
   ├── Multi-Term Exam Scheduling (Qawmi Chala & Alia Semester)
   ├── Subject Marks Entry with Automatic Grace & Fail Calculations
   ├── Immutable Historical Result Snapshots (Archival Integrity)
   ├── Trilingual Marksheets & Merit List Generation (PDF)
   └── Verifiable Sanad & Transcript Engine with Public QR Verification

8. Finance & Double-Entry Accounting
   ├── Configurable Fee Categories & Billing Rules
   ├── Automated Monthly Student Invoice Generation
   ├── Payment Collection (Cash Counterfoil & MFS: bKash, Nagad)
   ├── Double-Entry General Ledger (Chart of Accounts, Balance Sheet)
   └── Segregated Shariah-Compliant Lillah & Zakat Sub-Ledgers

9. Darul Iqamah (Hostel & Boarding) Management
   ├── Room, Floor, and Bed Allocation Registry
   ├── Residential vs Non-Residential Student Tracking
   ├── Daily Mess Meal Count & Kitchen Headcount Calculator
   └── Overnight Student Leave & Gate-Pass Registry

10. Institutional Website CMS & Communication
    ├── Dynamic Public Madrasa Portal (Noticeboard, About, Faculty, Gallery)
    ├── Online Student Admission Application Portal
    ├── Multilingual Public Notice Publishing (Bangla, English, Arabic)
    ├── Provider-Agnostic SMS & Notification Engine
    └── Secure Document & Certificate Public Verification Gateway
```
